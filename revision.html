REVISION
POLICY.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. What is a revision?

A revision means a reasonable change to work based on the original project requirements.

2. Included revisions

The number of revisions depends on the service and the agreement made before the project begins.

3. New requirements

A request that significantly changes the original project, adds new sections or requires additional work may be treated as a new request and may involve an additional charge.

4. Customer feedback

Customers should provide clear and specific feedback. Providing all important feedback together can help avoid unnecessary delays.

5. Final approval

Once the customer approves the final work, further changes may be treated as additional work.
