UNEMPLOYED / REFUNDS

REFUND
POLICY.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Before payment

Customers should confirm the service, requirements, price and expected delivery before payment.

2. Custom work

Because many UNEMPLOYED services involve customized work, refunds may not be available after work has substantially started or after the final work has been delivered.

3. Eligible refund situations

A refund may be considered when UNEMPLOYED is unable to provide the agreed service or when a refund has been specifically agreed upon before the project begins.

4. Customer cancellation

If a customer cancels after work has started, the amount refundable, if any, will depend on the work already completed and the agreement made for the project.

5. Incorrect information

Refunds are generally not provided for problems caused by incorrect, incomplete or misleading information supplied by the customer.

6. Requesting a refund

Refund requests should be submitted through the contact method provided on the website with the relevant order information and reason for the request.
