UNEMPLOYED / DELIVERY

DELIVERY
POLICY.

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Digital delivery

UNEMPLOYED services are generally delivered digitally. Depending on the service, completed work may be provided through email, messaging or another agreed digital method.

2. Delivery time

Delivery time depends on the service, project complexity, customer requirements and current workload. An estimated delivery time will be discussed before work begins.

3. Customer delays

Delivery may be delayed when required information, materials, feedback or approvals are not provided on time.

4. Final files

The format of the final files will depend on the service requested and what was agreed upon for the project.

5. Delivery problems

If a customer does not receive the agreed digital delivery, they should contact UNEMPLOYED so the issue can be checked and resolved where possible.
